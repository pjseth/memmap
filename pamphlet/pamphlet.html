How this works

For Julia’s 25th birthday, everyone who shared her 24th year was asked one thing: pick a place you went with her, write a short note, and send a photo if you have one.

40 people answered, from her rooftop in Chinatown to a seafood buffet in Ginza. Together they make a map of the year.

Every place has a number. Find it on the poster, then find it here to read what was written there. The journey starts at home, wanders across New York, heads out of the city, and then goes around the world.
